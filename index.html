How payday works

Four steps. No wallet connect, no form that goes nowhere.

01Hold

Hold $UBI in a wallet you can post from. The minimum is small and it's printed on this page.

02Register your handle

Post your GTA handle and platform from the X account that holds. One handle per account.

03Every day, 18:00 UTC

The day's fees buy official cash cards. Codes go out by DM to as many handles as the pot covers; the rest roll into a draw.

04Payslip on the wall

Every payment is a line on the public ledger: date, handle, amount, proof. Never the code.

Talk to payroll

Straight answers.

What is UBI?

A coin with a payroll. Every trade of $UBI pays a creator fee. Every day at 18:00 UTC the fees buy official in-game cash cards, and the codes go out to the GTA handles registered on the payroll. Every payment is logged publicly.

How do I get on the payroll?

Hold $UBI, then post your GTA handle and platform from the X account you hold with. The register button writes the post for you. One handle per account.

Does everyone get paid every day?

As many as the pot covers, in registration order, then the rest roll into a draw. The pot is public, the order is public, the draw is posted. It's basic income, not guaranteed income.

Is this a return or a dividend?

No. Paydays are gifts of in-game cash paid for by fees. They are not a return, not a dividend, not a promise, and holding gives you a place on the payroll, not a claim on it.

Which platforms?

PC, PlayStation and Xbox. Codes are platform-specific and some platforms restrict codes by region; the payroll says what it can deliver before payday.

Is this affiliated with Rockstar or Take-Two?

No. UBI is an independent fan project. Cash cards are bought from official stores at the published price. Game names and marks belong to their owners.

Is this advice?

No. Memecoins are extremely risky and most go to zero. Nothing here is advice.
