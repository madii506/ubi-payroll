Notices

Straight answers

What is UBI?

A coin with a payroll. Every trade of $UBI pays a creator fee. Every day at 18:00 UTC the fees buy official in-game cash cards, and the codes go out to the GTA handles registered on the payroll. Every payment is a public record.

Does everyone get paid every day?

As many as the pot covers, in registration order, then the rest roll into a draw. The pot is public, the order is public, the draw is posted. Basic income, not guaranteed income.

Is this a return or a dividend?

No. Paydays are gifts of in-game cash paid for by fees. Not a return, not a dividend, not a promise. Holding gives you a place on the payroll, not a claim on it.

Is this affiliated with Rockstar or Take-Two?

No. UBI is an independent fan project. Cash cards are bought from official stores at the published price. Game names and marks belong to their owners.

Is this advice?

No. Memecoins are extremely risky and most go to zero. Nothing here is advice.
